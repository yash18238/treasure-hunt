Quest of the Lost Isle

Find the key.
Open the chest.

Explore a foggy island, collect clues and coins, avoid hidden mud traps and reach the treasure before your moves run out.

Choose difficulty

How to play

  • ↑←↓→ or WASD to move
  • Each step costs 1 move. Boulders and the sea block you.
  • Pick up scrolls to learn where the chest is hidden.
  • Watch the hot / cold meter - it shows how close you are.
  • You need the golden key to open the chest.
  • Press H for a hint (costs 3 moves). Clear 3 levels to win!
